FAQ

Common questions

Do you install solar systems?

Yes. DESS designs and installs solar and backup power systems, including panels, inverters and battery storage, configured to your requirements.

Do you install CCTV systems for businesses?

Yes. We design and install CCTV systems for offices, shops, hotels, schools, hospitals, factories and estates, including DVR/NVR recording and remote viewing setup.

Do you provide CCTV maintenance?

Yes. We inspect, troubleshoot, repair where applicable and upgrade existing CCTV systems.

Can DESS handle large commercial projects?

DESS is equipped and positioned to take on commercial and institutional projects, from planning and system design to installation, configuration and support. Share your requirements and we will discuss the scope with you.

Do you serve Abuja?

Yes. Our Mararaba base serves Nasarawa, Abuja and surrounding areas.

Do you serve outside Kano and Abuja?

Yes. DESS can take on projects nationwide and outside Nigeria depending on requirements. Pricing may vary with location, logistics and scope.

Do you install Starlink?

Yes. We handle Starlink installation, activation and configuration, connectivity setup and technical support.

Do you provide maintenance after installation?

Yes. Maintenance and technical support are a core DESS service for solar, CCTV, connectivity and other installed systems.

Can DESS provide technical consultation?

Yes. We can advise on the right solution before you buy equipment or begin installation.

Do you work with hotels, schools and hospitals?

We are equipped to serve these sectors. Requirements differ between them, so we begin by understanding your premises and needs.
