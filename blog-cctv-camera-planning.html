How many CCTV cameras do you need? A simple planning guide

There is no magic number of cameras. Here is how to plan coverage around your property instead.

13 October 2026 · 3 min read

There is no single right number of cameras. The right number depends on your property, your risks and what you want to see. Plan coverage first, then count cameras.

Walk the property, day and night

List every entrance and exit, the perimeter, parking, storage, reception or cash points and any dark corners. Note where lighting is poor, because night footage depends on it.

Rank your priorities

Start with the places where a problem would hurt most, usually entrances, gates and valuable stock or equipment. Then extend coverage outward so you do not leave blind spots between cameras.

Think about field of view and height

A wide camera covers more area but shows less detail. Cameras at gates and doors usually need to capture faces or vehicle details, so they need a closer view. Mount cameras out of easy reach and protect them from weather.

Plan the recorder and storage

More cameras, higher resolution and longer retention all need more storage. Decide how many days of footage you want to keep and size the recorder and hard drive for it.

Power, cabling and remote viewing

Plan cable routes and a reliable power supply. If you want to view cameras on your phone, you will need a stable internet connection at the site.

Respect privacy

Avoid filming private areas such as toilets or a neighbour's property, and consider placing signs where cameras operate.
